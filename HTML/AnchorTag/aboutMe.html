About Us

Naresh i Technologies is an IT training institute that offers training in various software technologies. It aims to help students, freshers, and working professionals improve their technical knowledge and develop skills required for the IT industry.
The institute provides training in programming languages, web development, database management, and full-stack development. Students can learn technical concepts through practical exercises and coding practice.

Our objectives:

  1. To provide quality technical education.
  2. To develop programming and problem-solving skills.
  3. To help students understand real-world software development.
  4. To encourage continuous learning and professional growth.
  5. 1. To prepare students for technical interviews and career opportunities.
