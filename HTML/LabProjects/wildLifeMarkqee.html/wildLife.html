Welcome to the Wild!

Wildlife is the beautiful world of animals, birds and other living creatures.

Let's explore the amazing creatures that make our planet special.

Every creature deserves a place on Earth!

🐾 MAMMALS

Mammals are warm-blooded animals. Most mammals give birth to young ones and feed them with milk.

🐅 TIGER

Tigers are powerful wild cats known for their orange fur and black stripes.

🦁 LION

Lions are known as the kings of the jungle. They usually live in groups called prides.

🐘 ELEPHANT

Elephants are the largest land animals. They have long trunks and large ears.

🦌 DEER

Deer are graceful animals commonly found in forests and grasslands.

🦜 BEAUTIFUL BIRDS

Birds are fascinating creatures with feathers, wings and beaks.

🦚 PEACOCK

The peacock is famous for its colorful and beautiful feathers.

🦜 PARROT

Parrots are colorful birds. Some species can imitate human sounds.

🦅 EAGLE

Eagles are powerful birds known for their sharp eyesight.

🐊 AMAZING REPTILES

Reptiles are cold-blooded animals with scales.

🐊 CROCODILE

Crocodiles are powerful reptiles that live in water and on land.

🐍 SNAKE

Snakes are reptiles without legs. They move by sliding across surfaces.

🐢 TURTLE

Turtles have protective shells that help protect their bodies.
